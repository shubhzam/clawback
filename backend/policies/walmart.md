# Walmart deduction policy (illustrative, synthetic text for demo use)

## 1.1 Dispute window
Suppliers may dispute a deduction within 30 calendar days of the deduction date. Disputes filed after the window closes are not reviewed and the deduction is treated as accepted.

## 2.1 Shortage claims
A shortage deduction is valid only where the quantity received on the signed proof of delivery is lower than the quantity shipped on the bill of lading. A signed proof of delivery showing full receipt is conclusive evidence against a shortage claim.

## 2.2 Damage claims
Damage must be noted on the proof of delivery at the time of receipt. Damage not annotated on the proof of delivery at delivery is not deductible.

## 2.3 Pricing claims
Price variance deductions are measured against the unit price on the purchase order. Where the invoiced unit price equals the purchase order unit price, no price deduction applies.

## 2.4 Delivery window compliance
Late delivery fines apply only to deliveries received outside the confirmed appointment window on the bill of lading. Deliveries within the appointment window are compliant.

## 3.1 Duplicate deductions
The same invoice, reason code and amount may be deducted only once. A repeat deduction is invalid and must be reversed.

## 3.2 Promotional and trade deductions
Promotional allowances require the signed trade agreement. Deductions without a matching agreement on file are escalated for commercial review.
