# Kroger deduction policy (illustrative, synthetic text for demo use)

## 1.1 Dispute window
Disputes must be received within 21 days of the deduction date. Late disputes are closed without review.

## 2.1 Shortage claims
Shortage deductions require the receiving report to show less than the quantity on the bill of lading. A clean signed receiving report voids the claim.

## 2.2 Damage claims
Damage must be documented on the receiving report at delivery. Later damage reports are not accepted.

## 2.3 Pricing claims
Price discrepancies are resolved against the purchase order. Matching invoice and purchase order prices mean no deduction is owed.

## 2.4 Delivery window compliance
Appointment fines are assessed only for receipts outside the booked window on the bill of lading.

## 3.1 Duplicate deductions
Duplicate deductions for the same invoice, reason code and amount are invalid and will be reversed.

## 3.2 Promotional and trade deductions
Promotional allowances need the signed agreement and are reviewed manually.
