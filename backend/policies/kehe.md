# KeHE deduction policy (illustrative, synthetic text for demo use)

## 1.1 Dispute window
Vendors have 45 days from the deduction date to submit a dispute with supporting documents.

## 2.1 Shortage claims
Shortages are validated against receiving records. If the signed receiving record matches the quantity on the bill of lading, the shortage deduction is reversed.

## 2.2 Damage claims
Damaged product must be recorded on the receiving record on the day of delivery. Unrecorded damage is the distributor's responsibility.

## 2.3 Pricing claims
Cost differences are calculated from the purchase order cost. If invoice cost matches purchase order cost the vendor owes no price adjustment.

## 2.4 Delivery window compliance
Appointment compliance is judged against the scheduled appointment window on the bill of lading. In-window receipts are not subject to late fees.

## 3.1 Duplicate deductions
Duplicate deductions for the same invoice and reason are reversed on request with the original deduction reference.

## 3.2 Promotional and trade deductions
Promotional deductions are reviewed against the signed promotional agreement and cannot be auto-validated.
